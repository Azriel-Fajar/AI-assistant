## The Mechanic — Follow + WA Confirm

1. They see ad / post: "Follow @rielcode + chat WA, dapat demo gratis + 10% off, promo Merdeka."
2. They follow socials (IG / TikTok / FB).
3. They WhatsApp Rielcode to confirm followed → unlock.
4. Rielcode replies: confirms, asks their business/need (NOT price dump — see [[feedback_wa_greeting_short_ask_need]]), offers free demo.
5. Free demo built → if proceed, MERDEKA10 applied.

**Why WA confirm not auto-code:** Rielcode is WhatsApp-primary, manual. WA confirm = conversation starts in the close channel. The follow is lead-capture; the discount is bait; the DM is the actual funnel.

**Enforcement:** Don't police follows hard. If they WA you, they're warm — that's the win. Asking them to follow first just grows the account on the way in.
