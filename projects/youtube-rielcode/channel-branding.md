```

Center text (huge, bold, sans-serif):
   RIELCODE
   Subline (smaller, lighter): Websites that get clients.

Right side (small, top-right): rielcode.com
```

```
Background: Dark gradient (black → dark blue)
Left half: bold text "I BUILD SITES." with smaller "for small businesses that need to convert."
Right half: 3 small thumbnails (Parallaxnet, Indonesian client, portfolio) arranged in tilted stack
Bottom-right: rielcode.com badge

```

```
Top-right corner: small Rielcode logo
```

- Direct, no filler ("just," "really," "actually")
- One idea per sentence
- No emojis on-screen or in copy
- No em dashes
- English (primary). If Bahasa later, rewrite, never translate
- Always end with one CTA, not two
