## Channel Identity

- **Handle:** @rielcodeofficial (matches IG, consistent brand)
- **Channel name:** Rielcode
- **Bio (under 100 chars):** "I build websites that get clients. SMB tips, real builds, no fluff. Free site audit: rielcode.com"
- **Banner concept:** Dark mode, "Rielcode | Websites that work" + 3 small thumbnails of past builds + URL.
- **Pinned video:** First build showcase (Parallaxnet Canada reveal) — best intro short.
- **Link-in-bio:** rielcode.com (homepage with /packages visible above fold)
- **Channel trailer:** 30s self-intro short — "I'm Azriel, I build sites for SMBs in 3-14 days. Here's what I post."

## Channel Vision

- **Angle:** Hybrid — builds (60%) + SMB tips (40%)
- **Language:** English
- **Format:** Shorts only (60s max) for first 3 months. Re-evaluate adding longs at Q3 review.
- **Production:** No face (no webcam). Screen rec + AI voiceover + bold text overlays.
- **Goal:** Lead-gen funnel → rielcode.com → /packages → order-form
- **Target audience:** SMB owners (international preferred), Indonesian SMBs secondary
- **North-star metric:** rielcode.com referral traffic from YouTube (track via UTM)

## Content Pillars

### Pillar 1 — Build Showcases (30%)
Before/after, client wins, transformation reveals. Real proof = trust.

Example topics:
- "I rebuilt this Canada client's site in 5 days. Here's what changed."
- "From IDR 500k to IDR 50M/mo: how a Salatiga cafe used their new site"
- "Watch me clone any site in 60 seconds"

Hook template: "I just [built/rebuilt/cloned] [X] for [Y]. Here's [the result/the trick/the catch]."

### Pillar 2 — SMB Website Tips (30%)
Why X feature matters, mistakes to avoid, ROI math.

Example topics:
- "Why your cafe needs a website (not just Instagram)"
- "3 mistakes that kill your contact form conversion"
- "The IDR 200k upgrade that 3x'd a client's WhatsApp leads"

Hook template: "Most [SMB type] do [common thing] wrong. Here's why."

### Pillar 3 — Process / Behind-Scenes (20%)
How a site gets built, tools, day-in-life. Demystifies value.

Example topics:
- "Day in my life building a $295 website"
- "My exact workflow from DM to delivery"
- "The 4 tools I use to build any site in under 7 days"

Hook template: "Watch me [do X] from scratch in [time]."

### Pillar 4 — Hot Takes / Controversy (20%)
Industry opinions, myth-busting, trends.

Example topics:
- "Wix is killing your business. Here's why."
- "Why I refuse to build sites with WordPress"
- "Stop paying agencies IDR 50M for landing pages"

Hook template: "[Common belief] is [dead/wrong/a scam]."

## Posting Cadence

| Day | Action | Why |
|---|---|---|
| **Sun** | Batch day: record + edit week's worth | Solo + college load = batching mandatory |
| **Mon** | Post #1 (start week strong) | Highest YT view day |
| **Wed** | Post #2 (midweek) | Algo refresh |
| **Fri** | Post #3 (lead into weekend) | SMB owners scroll YT on weekends |
| Tue/Sat | Optional stretch posts | Only if batch had bandwidth |

**Baseline:** 3/week. **Stretch:** 5/week. **Floor:** 2/week (never below).
