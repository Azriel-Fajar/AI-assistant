## CAUTION FLAGS
1. **SEO is not instant.** New domain (jatrascale.com) takes ~2-4 months to rank, and only with consistent article publishing. If he expects leads next week, he needs Google Ads, not SEO. Tell him upfront so he is not disappointed.
2. **Blog is required for the ranking goal, not optional.** Ranking for "jasa kalibrasi timbangan", "service jembatan timbang" etc comes from published content. No blog = weak ranking.
3. **Real content needed for credibility.** Placeholder logo/images fine to launch, but real project photos + real testimonials needed eventually to convert industrial buyers.
4. **ChatGPT set his scope expectation, not us.** He may anchor to a premium feature list at a budget price. ChatGPT does not know our tiers.
