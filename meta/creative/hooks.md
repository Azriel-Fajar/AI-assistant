# Hook Bank — Rielcode WhatsApp Ads (v3)

Step 6 of the viral system. One concept (trust bullseye), 10-12 hook variations. Launch 1-2, rotate next when frequency climbs / CPM rises. Keeps the concept alive for months instead of dying in a week.

Render rules (locked): fast-paced, max 3s/scene, new redesign theme.ts (cream + forest green), customer-POV VO, no em dashes, brisk natural read. Keep CTA "Send WhatsApp Message", destination WhatsApp, audience ID 18-65.

Each hook = first 0-3s. Body and CTA stay roughly constant (proof + price + WA). Hook type tagged.
