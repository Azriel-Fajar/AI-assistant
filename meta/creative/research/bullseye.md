## The Bullseye (working hypothesis — refine once quotes are in)

**One core problem:** Local business owners look less trustworthy than they are, because IG-only makes them look small/temporary, and customers asking "ada websitenya?" walk away.

Everything else is a symptom of this:
- "looks unprofessional" → symptom
- "hard to find on Google" → symptom
- "competitor has a site" → symptom
- "can't show full catalog" → symptom

**Wrap every ad around:** trust. The website is the thing that makes a real customer believe you're a real, established business before they even message.
