# Swipe Database

Attention-grabber library. Every thumbnail / title that hooked Azriel, logged for reuse in Rielcode ads.

How it fills: Azriel screenshots anything that grabs him. Drops PNG in `thumbnails/` or `titles/`, OR pastes in chat. Claude logs the row below.

**Pattern type:**
- **match** = visual cue prospect already knows (triggers identity, "this is for me")
- **interrupt** = wild/outrageous, stops the scroll ("what is this?")

When logging, capture WHY it hooked (the mechanism), not just "looks nice".
