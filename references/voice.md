# Voice Reference

Match this register when drafting client messages or outreach. Do not fake voice on external content without showing Azriel first.

## Sample 1 -- English (client follow-up, WhatsApp)
```
Hey Ali, I wanted to follow up on the website I uploaded a couple of days ago. Did you and your team get a chance to review it? Let me know what you think.
```

## Sample 2 -- Indonesian (local lead follow-up, WhatsApp)
```
selamat sore kak, hanya ingin memastikan, bagaimana dengan review portfolio yang saya beri kemarin?
Tidak apa jika masih ingin melihat-lihat, jika kakak ada pertanyaan apapun, bisa langsung tanya saja ya kak😄
```

## Voice notes
- English: casual, direct, no pressure. Short sentences. Warm but professional.
- Indonesian: polite, soft tone, uses "kak" as respectful address. Low-pressure, gives the lead room to breathe.
- Both: no formality, no hard sell, no long paragraphs.
