# Azriel's WA Message Tone Guide

## The Voice

Casual, warm, short. Feels like a real person texting, not a business replying.

## Approved Example (from Azriel, 2026-06-05)

> wah keren kak,
> kira kira website seperti apa yang bisa kamu bantu untuk bisnis kakak?

## What This Example Shows

- Lowercase throughout, even sentence starts
- "wah keren kak" = genuine reaction, not fake hype
- No emojis
- No filler phrases ("Terima kasih sudah menghubungi kami", "Kami siap membantu", etc.)
- Skips self-promotion entirely, goes straight to the lead's need
- "kira kira" = low-pressure, inviting, not interrogating
- Short question at the end, open-ended, lets the lead define what they want
- Feels like talking to a person, not a business

## Approved Example 2 (from Azriel, 2026-06-05) -- feature list

> untuk website tour dan travel, fitur yang kami sarankan antara lain:
> 1. halaman paket wisata lengkap dengan harga dan itinerary
> 2. form pemesanan atau inquiry
> 3. galeri foto destinasi
> 4. testimoni pelanggan
> 5. tombol WA biar calon pelanggan langsung bisa hubungi kak
> ada fitur tertentu yang kak mau prioritaskan?

- "kami sarankan" not "kami buatkan" -- suggest, don't assume
- numbered list OK for features, keeps it readable on mobile
- ends with question to keep lead engaged

## Rules Derived From This

- Use lowercase naturally (not forced)
- "kak" = address/greeting ("halo kak", "baik kak"); "kakak" = referring to them in a sentence ("supaya calon pelanggan bisa menghubungi kakak")
- No company name mention unless necessary
- No service list -- ask what THEY need first
- One short reaction + one question = ideal structure for early-stage leads
- Never explain what Rielcode does unprompted at this stage
- Let the lead describe their vision before pitching anything
