## Layout on 1080×1920

Kicker y≈200 at x=57, mono 25 px, spaced caps. Title under it, heavy 55 px, lower-case plain statement. Caption one or two dark rounded rows centred at y≈1300, 53 px: said white, current on a yellow chip, unsaid 42% white. Tags: yellow pills in mono caps with a thin leader and dot. Cards: cream, rounded, optional black outline, mono kicker. Top ~25% dissolves into the page colour. Nothing important in the bottom ~20%.

## Writing patterns

- Opens mid-conflict with a sound: "Plung!", "Nguuung", or a price contrast. No greeting, no channel name.
- Folk belief vs authority, or cheap vs expensive; then a fixed sign-off line by second 7.
- Everyday comparisons and hedged numbers ("sekitar", "hampir", "±"); the limits are shown: "hitungan kami", "contoh", "uji kecil, 2014".
- Fairness turn: "Jadi yang murah salah? Nggak juga."
- Ends with a checklist and one line that restates the surprise. Monetises by numbered catalogue links with an affiliate disclosure.
- Top topics are money and safety on things people own: motor, bensin, AC, listrik, HP. Questions in titles, one or two figures in the title.

## Where this skill differs on purpose

Procedural kit parts rather than licensed models, so any object can be built from scratch with no asset hunt and a render that fits a laptop without a GPU. The cost: a complex shape such as a whole motorbike looks simpler than their GLB models. If a topic needs one, a CC-licensed GLB (with credit) can be loaded with three's GLTFLoader, as they do.

Captions differ too: the reference uses karaoke rows on dark chips; this skill shows big 1–3 word chunks with an outline (Azriel's choice, 1 Nov 2026). Camera moves are not blurred either, unlike the reference's smeared moves, so nobody gets dizzy.
