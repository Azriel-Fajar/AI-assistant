# Keranjang kuning rules

What TikTok Shop by Tokopedia's Indonesian policy pages say, read directly on 1 Nov 2026. These pages change. If the date above is older than 30 days, re-read the links, update this file and tell Azriel what changed.

|---|---|---|

## Not settled

- **Is a fully animated product film "clearly shown"?** The pages don't say. Safest plan: a short real clip or real photo of the exact product beside the animation. Every suggestion states how the product will be shown, and says if Azriel would have to buy or borrow it.
- **AI voice in product videos.** The Indonesian pages don't ban it. A US TikTok Shop document was [reported in May 2026](https://ppc.land/tiktok-shops-quality-rules-ban-ai-voices-and-still-images-from-lives/) to ban AI voices, with the detail written around LIVE sessions. The Gemini narrator (Achird) stays for now. Recheck before the first keranjang upload, because a rule like this can spread to other markets.
- **Followers needed for the keranjang.** The original author's app said on 1 Nov 2026 that the showcase stays hidden until he has 600 followers, a post in the past 28 days and no Community Guidelines problem. The screen names the showcase only; media say the same bar applies to the keranjang on videos.

## Choice on weaknesses (original author's, confirm with Azriel)

The film shows advantages only; weaknesses go in the report to Azriel (original author's decision, 1 Nov 2026). That still has limits. Every advantage in the film must be true of the linked model and backed by a source. If a weakness is a safety problem, or would make the film's main promise misleading, the product is dropped rather than filmed around.
