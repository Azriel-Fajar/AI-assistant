## TL;DR

Parallaxnet Indonesia is a real, actively-operating US-linked IT-education (EdTech) brand. Genuine mainstream press coverage (Kompas, Media Indonesia, ANTARA) and many verifiable university/school MoUs. No scam reports found. Main gaps: no public legal-registration data (NIB/OSS), and an unclear two-entity structure (PT Parallaxnet Siber Indonesia vs distributor PT Kimora Defasa). The "Parallaxnet Canada" entity from your client notes could NOT be independently verified online.

## 1. Identity & Legal Entity

- **Indonesian entity:** PT. Parallaxnet Siber Indonesia (Parallaxnet Cyber Indonesia). Footer: "© 2026 PT. Parallaxnet Siber Indonesia. All rights reserved."
- **Parent / brand:** US-based Parallaxnet (parallaxnet.com). HQ: **600 W 7th St, Los Angeles, CA 90017**.
- **Possible distributor:** Multiple press sources name **PT Kimora Defasa** as Parallaxnet's "sole distributor in Indonesia." Relationship between the two Indonesian vehicles is not clarified publicly. **Flag: unclear corporate structure.**
- **Not found:** NIB, OSS, AHU/Kemenkumham registration, registered street address, director list.

## 2. Business Model

- Online IT-education / EdTech platform.
- Tagline: **"Merevolusi Pendidikan dengan Pembelajaran IT Praktis"** (Revolutionizing education with practical IT learning).
- Indonesia motto: **"No Indonesian Children Left Behind."**
- Delivery: Moodle-based LMS (`learn.parallaxnet.com`) + hands-on cloud lab VM.
- Each student gets a **VM (2 vCore, 2GB RAM, 25GB), 100+ to ~300 open-source enterprise apps, usable up to 1–2 years**, plus an **"International Parallaxnet USA Certification"** (claimed no expiry).
- **Mission:** "Parallaxnet berkomitmen melahirkan generasi inovatif dan siap masa depan, melalui pembelajaran praktis yang mengintegrasikan teknologi dan bisnis di era digital."
- **Vision:** graduates who understand the symbiosis of technology and business in the digital world.

## 10. Trust / Risk Signals

  - Aggressive "international certification" + free-VM marketing to students/schools warrants normal buyer due diligence.
