When sending a demo website link to a lead/customer, the WA message must state the design is just a rough draft and can be customized to their taste.

**Why:** Manages expectation. Lead should not think the demo is final. Lowers pressure, invites feedback, keeps door open for their preferences.

**How to apply:** Add a line like "Ini masih desain kasar ya kak, tampilannya bisa disesuaikan sesuai selera kakak" near the close, alongside the existing "foto/teks masih contoh" note. Keep professional, no em dashes. See [[feedback_follow_up_no_em_dashes]], [[project_demo_sites]].
