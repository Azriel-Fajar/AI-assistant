Rielcode's bottleneck is NOT lead generation. It is follow-up and closing.

As of 2026-06-10: 4 lead channels live (YT, Meta Ads, audit tool, outreach), Rp 404k spent, 0 new clients closed. Grill exposed the leak is post-demo: lead asks for a niche example, gets a live demo link from demo.rielcode.com, then ghosts. No follow-up sent, no close script.

**Why:** Azriel defaults to building MORE lead-gen (new channels, more ads) to avoid the harder manual step of following up and pushing leads to a next step. Time is constrained by college, which makes passive channels feel safer than active closing.

**How to apply:** When Azriel asks how to get more leads/clients, do NOT default to new channels or more ad budget. First ask about close rate and follow-up. Push him toward: a 3-step WA close script (demo link, ask goal, propose DP/call) and Day-3 follow-up reminders per lead. Only scale spend once close rate is above 0.
