When reviewing Meta ads, NEVER recommend un-pausing v1/v2 or switching to them because their cost-per-conversation looks cheaper. v1 (WA Lead Gen) and v2 route messages to **Messenger**, not WhatsApp. v3 routes to **WhatsApp**, which is the channel Azriel wants because WA is his primary client comms and the friction filters tire-kickers. The cheaper Messenger CPL is a vanity metric (spam/no real buyers).

**Why:** I recommended "un-pause v1, pause v3" on 2026-06-12 purely on CPM/cost-per-convo math, ignoring [[project_meta_ads_v3_finding]] and [[project_meta_ads_v1_v3_channels]] which already documented this. Azriel had to correct the same mistake twice.

**How to apply:**
- v3 staying live is correct. Don't touch the channel.
- Optimize for cost-per-qualified-lead / cost-per-close, not cost-per-convo.
- Real fix is the close + fresh creative + CPM reduction, never "go back to Messenger."
