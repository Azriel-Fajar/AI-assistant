Azriel is running a Meta Ads campaign targeting local audience to generate WhatsApp leads for Rielcode.

**Campaign details (as of 2026-05-31, day 2):**
- Campaign name: Rielcode - WA Lead Gen
- Objective: Engagement (messaging conversations started)
- Daily budget: IDR 18,000/day
- Target: Local audience (not limited to Salatiga)
- Opportunity score: 100/100, 0 recommendations from Meta

**Performance after 2 days:**
- 5 messaging conversations started
- Cost per messaging conversation: IDR 5,484
- Total spent: IDR 27,420
- All 5 leads went cold after initial exchange

**The 5 leads (all cold as of 2026-05-31):**
- Ilda Yti -- asked "Can I learn more about your business?"
- Apif Apipah -- asked "Can I learn more about your business?"
- Faiz Faisal -- asked "Can I learn more about your business?" (messaged 05:57, replied 7+ hours later)
- Bape Ne Faisal -- asked "Can you tell me more about your ad?"
- Rudy R -- asked "Can you tell me more about your ad?"

**Why they went cold:**
- Auto-reply was an info dump with no question -- nothing to respond to
- Two leads (Bape Ne Faisal, Rudy R) received price (IDR 500,000 / $30) before value was established
- Faiz had a 7+ hour reply gap -- too slow for warm leads
- All 5 messaged in English; Indonesian auto-reply would have been a mismatch

**Auto-reply fix (new template):**
Use English. End with one open question. No price in the first message.

For "Can I learn more about your business?":
> Hey! Thanks for reaching out. Rielcode builds professional websites -- landing pages, online stores, or custom business sites. What kind of business do you have?

For "Can you tell me more about your ad?":
> Hey! We help businesses get online with a clean, professional website -- from landing pages to full online stores. What kind of website are you looking for?

**Re-engagement message sent to all 5 leads:**
> Halo lagi! Mau mastiin aja, ada yang bisa aku bantu? Konsultasinya gratis, santai aja.

**Budget advice:**
- IDR 18k/day is performing well -- do not raise yet
- Wait until day 7 before any budget decisions
- If scaling, raise by 20-30% max every 3-5 days to avoid resetting learning phase
- Only raise if messages stop coming in and more volume is needed
