Client/lead WA drafts must be professional, clear, and plain. Do not open with customer-pleasing hype like "wah pas banget kak", "cocok banget kak", "wah menarik". State facts and answer the need directly.

**Why:** Azriel dislikes constant pleasing tone. Wants easy-to-understand professional replies, not flattery.

**How to apply:** Open with a plain acknowledgment like "Baik kak," then answer the need directly. No hype openers. Example: instead of "Wah cocok banget kak!" use "Baik kak, website ini cocok untuk usaha kakak." Still keep "kami" not "saya" ([[feedback_wa_use_kami]]), warm "kak", no em dashes, plain copy-paste block. Extends [[feedback_wa_no_hype_opener]] and [[feedback_azriel_message_tone]].
