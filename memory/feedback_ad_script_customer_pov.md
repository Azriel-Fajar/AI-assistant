  type: feedback
---

All Rielcode ad scripts and voiceovers must be written from the **customer's point of view**: what value they receive, what they get out of it. Not "aku buat", "kami bantu", "aku kirim" (seller POV). Use "kamu dapat", "usaha kamu punya", "pelanggan kamu" (benefit to them).

Example (good, customer POV):
"Bayangin usaha kamu punya website yang bikin pelanggan langsung percaya. Calon pembeli cari kamu di Google, ketemu, terus langsung chat."

**Why:** First draft was seller POV ("ini yang sudah aku buat"). Azriel wants the message delivered from the audience's perspective: what they gain, the value handed to them.

**How to apply:** Lead every ad beat with the customer's benefit or outcome. CTA can still say "chat WhatsApp" but the body sells what THEY get. Casual kak tone, no em dashes [[feedback_azriel_message_tone]] [[feedback_remotion_fast_paced_3s_frame]].
