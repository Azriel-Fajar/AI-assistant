Rielcode's WhatsApp Business auto-greeting for Meta Ads leads must stay SHORT. Just greet and ask what their business is + what they need. Do NOT dump price, package list, or demo links in the first auto-reply. Too much info on first contact overwhelms the lead.

Approved greeting:
"Halo kak, makasih sudah hubungi Rielcode. Boleh cerita dulu usaha kak di bidang apa dan butuh website seperti apa?"

Send the matched demo link + price anchor + free-mockup offer at reply 2, AFTER they describe their business.

**Why:** I proposed a greeting with price + rielcode.com/demos link up front. Azriel rejected it: too much to take in on first message. He prefers ask-need-first, value-second.

**How to apply:** Keep first auto-reply to greet + one question. Front-load discovery, not the pitch. Demo link comes after they reply. [[project_meta_ads_wa_quick_replies]] [[feedback_azriel_message_tone]]
