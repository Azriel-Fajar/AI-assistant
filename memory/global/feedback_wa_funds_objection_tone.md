When a lead/customer says yes to scope but is still collecting funds ("lagi kumpulin dananya"), reply tone: low pressure, reassure scope already fits, tell them to ping when ready, say you'll save their details. Do NOT push to close.

NEVER mention their money/funds back to them. Do NOT say "tinggal nunggu dananya aja" or anything that frames the wait as about cash. That signals you're just reaching for their money, customers dislike it. Frame around scope fit and readiness instead.

Then offer the LAUNCH10 promo as a soft incentive: "kami juga kebetulan ada promo jika menggunakan kode LAUNCH10, kakak akan dapat diskon 10%".

**Why:** Azriel edited my draft to this tone, added LAUNCH10 himself, and corrected me for writing "tinggal nunggu dananya aja". Money objection = timing not price; naming their money hurts trust.

**How to apply:** Use on any "waiting on funds" reply. Lowercase, "kak", no em dashes. Never reference their funds. Mention LAUNCH10 10% discount when nudging a warm-but-not-yet-paying lead. Related: [[project_redesign_launch]] (LAUNCH10 promo), [[feedback_azriel_message_tone]].
