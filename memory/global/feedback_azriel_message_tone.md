Azriel's voice for casual messages and social captions: short, warm, low-key. NOT salesy or hyped.

**Reference example he wrote himself (WhatsApp opener):**
```
Halo kak, mau tanya soal website bisnis.
dari [nama]
```

**The tone:**
- "kak" = address/greeting ("halo kak", "baik kak"), "kakak" = referring to them in a sentence ("supaya calon pelanggan bisa menghubungi kakak")
- Short. One or two lines. No paragraphs.
- Lowercase-leaning, relaxed.
- Plain "dari [nama]" sign-off, not formal.
- NO hype words ("buruan!", "untung gue kenal!"), NO fake urgency, NO emoji spam.
- "lo/gue" reads tryhard/cringe to him — avoid unless he asks. "kamu"/"kak" preferred.

**Why:** He rejected hyped, templated, "lo/gue" captions as cringe. Wants natural human voice, not ad-speak. Found 2026-06-04.

**Additional approved examples (2026-06-05):**

WA opener to new lead:
```
wah keren kak,
kira kira website seperti apa yang bisa kamu bantu untuk bisnis kakak?
```

WA feature list pitch:
```
untuk website tour dan travel, fitur yang kami sarankan antara lain:
1. halaman paket wisata lengkap dengan harga dan itinerary
2. form pemesanan atau inquiry
3. galeri foto destinasi
4. testimoni pelanggan
5. tombol WA biar calon pelanggan langsung bisa hubungi kak
ada fitur tertentu yang kak mau prioritaskan?
```

**Extra rules from these examples:**
- "kami sarankan" not "kami buatkan" -- suggest, don't assume/presume
- Never open with "biasanya kami buatkan..." -- sounds like a template
- Numbered list OK for feature pitches, readable on mobile
- Always end with a question to keep lead engaged

**How to apply:** For any Rielcode caption, WA message, or referral copy, default to this casual-warm tone. Match this example's brevity and warmth. See [[project_meta_ads_v3_whatsapp]]. The `/caption` and `/follow-up` skills should follow this. Full tone guide: `.claude/skills/follow-up/references/tone-guide.md`.
