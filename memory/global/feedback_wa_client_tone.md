When generating WhatsApp/lead messages for Azriel's clients, match HIS voice (samples he pasted 2026-06-14):

- "kak" at END of sentence as address/closer (warm, respectful). "kakak" MID-sentence when referring to the person (e.g. "fitur yang kakak sebutin", "kalau kakak serius"). Never swap them.
- 🙏🏻 emoji on sensitive/soft points: price limits, things we can't do, polite asks. (Client WA only — overrides the internal no-emoji rule.)
- Short lines, ONE idea each. Split into separate sends for chatty back-and-forth, BUT send a structured comparison/list as ONE block (don't over-split).
- Keep it SHORT — client is lazy to read. Cut everything non-essential. No bold headers needed.
- Follow-up opener as own send: "halo kak, saya ingin follow up mengenai [topik]".
- Compress option compares to one line each: "1jt = ... . 2jt = ... " then a one-line saran + "kira-kira mau yang mana kak?".
- Lowercase, casual, no shouting caps.
- Soft bad-news delivery: open with "mau info saja kak", "saran saya", "tapi saran saya". Never blunt.
- Sell convenience over features: "ga perlu ribet lagi kak", "langsung bisa dipakai".
- Ask before assuming scope: "rencana ingin website seperti apa ya kak?", "ada referensi website yang kakak pengen?", "apakah rencana ingin hampir sama persis?".
- State process plainly then soften: "20% di awal untuk produksi, 80% untuk penyelesaian", "pembayaran lewat transfer atau qris".
- Point to spec link: https://rielcode.com/en/services
- No em dashes (existing rule [[feedback_follow_up_no_em_dashes]]).
