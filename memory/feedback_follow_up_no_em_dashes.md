Never use em dashes (--) in follow-up messages or any WhatsApp message drafts.

**Why:** Azriel explicitly flagged this. Em dashes feel unnatural in Indonesian WA messages.

**How to apply:** Replace em dashes with commas, periods, or rewrite the sentence. No exceptions.
