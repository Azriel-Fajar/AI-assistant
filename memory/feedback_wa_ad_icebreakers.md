The tap-to-send icebreakers (FAQ) on click-to-WhatsApp ads default to generic English ("Can I learn more about your business?"). Replace with Indonesian prompts that drive toward the demo close and qualify on price.

**Why:** Generic English defaults don't match the Indonesian greeting or push toward the demo (the thing that closes [[feedback_close_gap_not_leadgen]]). Icebreakers are written from customer POV (what the lead taps to ask the business). Use formal "saya" since the lead is asking a business [[feedback_followup_saya_tone]].

**How to apply (approved set):**
1. Mau lihat contoh website usaha saya
2. Berapa biayanya?
3. Berapa lama jadinya?
4. Gimana cara mulainya?

Edit in the ad's WhatsApp message template (Ads Manager, ad level) so all ads inherit. WA may cap at 3. #1 pulls to the demo, #2 surfaces Mulai Rp500rb early to filter tire-kickers.
