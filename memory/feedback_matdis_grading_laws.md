When grading Matdis equivalence-law assignments for Azriel's students:

- **Simplifikasi is a valid equivalence law.** p ∨ (¬p ∧ q) ≡ p ∨ q (and dual p ∧ (¬p ∨ q) ≡ p ∧ q). Accept it as a named law, full points.
- **Implikasi steps do not need the law name written.** p → q ≡ ¬p ∨ q is considered common knowledge. Never deduct the law point for a missing (or mislabeled) name on an implication step.

**Why:** I deducted points for "Simplifikasi" (called it an inference rule, not an equivalence law) and for unnamed Implikasi steps. Azriel corrected both on 2026-10-11.

**How to apply:** Re-check the law list before calling a student's law name invalid. Azriel's material also has "Hukum Ekuivalensi Tambahan" (biimplikasi, kontraposisi, eksportasi, ¬(p→q) ≡ p ∧ ¬q) given as formulas only; a law written as its formula counts as named.
